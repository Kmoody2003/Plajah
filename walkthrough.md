## 3. Hotkeys & Controls

- **Keys `1` – `9`, `0`, `-`, `=`**: Switch across the **12 3D Masterwork Installations**.
- **Keys `Q`, `W`, `E`, `R`, `T`, `Y`, `U`, `I`**: Switch across the **8 Image-Derived Visualizers** (Calm & Energetic pairs).
- **Spacebar**: Play / Pause audio playback.
- **`F` Key**: Toggle Fullscreen.
- **Audio Inputs**: Drag-and-drop local audio files, toggle Mic input, or run the built-in multi-stem Synth demo.
