Stillness fields

The five meditation shaders, live, driven by a real 20-minute session running at whatever speed you set. Session scrubs the arc — arrival through the turn to return — and the four uniforms follow from it exactly as they would on channel 8.1. Sound fakes the ensemble so you can see how much the audio moves things; at 0 the bands are silent, at 1 the drone, voice and breath are all singing. Arc speed scrubs the session faster than real time — it does not speed the visuals up, which always run at the rate they would on air.

—

Every field ends by passing its colour through safe(), which holds the luminance floor, caps saturated red, and desaturates by hue — warm content loses chroma as the session deepens, cool content keeps nearly all of it. That last rule is what changed: it used to pull everything toward grey, which is why these read as colourless.
