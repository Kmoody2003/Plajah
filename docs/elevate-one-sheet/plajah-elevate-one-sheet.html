[01] · 11 CFR § 110.11

Statutory “Paid for by…” Box Disclaimers

Auto-renders a bordered box (“PAID FOR BY [COMMITTEE NAME], [TREASURER], TREASURER. Authorized by [Candidate]…”) across campaign pages, Post Man emails, Reello lower-thirds, and Tela Studio PDFs.

[02] · 11 CFR § 104.7

Occupation, Employer & Street Address Gate

Requires Donor Full Legal Name, Physical Residential Street Address (rejects P.O. Boxes), Occupation, Employer, and the 5-Point Eligibility Attestation while enforcing per-donor election-cycle aggregate caps.

[03] · 26 U.S.C. § 6113 & 11 CFR § 100.52(d)

Non-Deductible Receipts + Stripe Pass-Through

Replaces 501(c)(3) tax receipts with statutory “Contributions or gifts are NOT tax-deductible” notices. 0% Plajah platform markup while standard Stripe processing fees (2.9% + $0.30) pass through directly to avoid any illegal corporate in-kind subsidy.

[04] · 26 U.S.C. § 501(c)(3)

IRS “Johnson Amendment” Coalition Firewall

Strictly blocks 501(c)(3) churches, charities, and cultural institutions from co-hosting a partisan 527 candidate drive or commingling member rosters.

[05] · 18 U.S.C. § 597

Anti-Vote-Buying Reward Guardrail

Records verified hours only on genuine volunteer labor shifts (canvassing, phonebanking, hosting a house party) — never on registering to vote or casting a ballot.
