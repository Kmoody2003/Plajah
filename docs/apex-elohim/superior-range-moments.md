## 2. Offer (wide shots: experiences the region must have)
- **Lake Superior coastal mountain highway**: a ledge road on cliffs over the lake, sea fog, sea stacks, a tunnel portal opening onto the coast, a lighthouse on a headland (6, 9, 12, 14, 24, 27, 29, 36, 41, 46, 49, 53, 62, 79, 80).
- **Escarpment ridge**: the road along and up the ridge with lookouts over the forest and lakes; a ridge lookout deck that doubles as a **festival stage** (16, 40, 51, 58).
- **Glacial summit**: snowfields, icy hairpins, an **ice arch** over the road (a natural glacial arch near the summit, the festival's signature landmark) (18, 23, 30, 52, 59).
- **Fjord canyons**: deep canyons with a river at the bottom, waterfalls off the walls, road ledges cut into the walls, and **canyon bridges**: steel arch (2), suspension (35, 75), a highway bridge past a waterfall (19), a wooden rail trestle (84), several bridges at different levels (20) (4, 45, 54, 55, 68).

- **Forest highways**: evergreen and autumn highways, river-side road in a gorge with rapids (0, 1, 4, 8, 10).

## 3b. Engineering rules still apply
Every road here is designed with the same rules as the rest of the map (road grade pass, scenic road designer, civic planner): grade limits by class (highway 6 %, mountain pass 8 %, gravel and tracks 12 %), curve radii sized for the design speed, banking from curvature, crests and sags rounded, cut and fill limits with structures (bridges, viaducts, tunnels, retaining walls) where the ground needs more. Bridges clear rivers and the fjord's boats; tunnels have portals and lighting; guardrails go on drop-offs and the outside of tight bends; every road joins the network (no orphan roads); utilities follow the corridors. The drama comes from the terrain and the views, never from a road that couldn't be built.

## 4. Wine country (before the foothills)
Holly-style glacial moraine ground (rolling hills, kettle lakes, wetlands, hardwood ridges) as the landform. Vineyards in long contour rows over the south-facing slopes, with a winery estate and tasting room on a rise. Flower farms on the flatter land in long single-colour bands, Holland (Veldheer) style: tulips, plus lavender and sunflower fields for summer, windmills, canals and footbridges. Country roads curve between them (no grid). Moments: driving between flower bands at golden hour, vineyard rows rolling over a crest, a windmill silhouette at sunset.
