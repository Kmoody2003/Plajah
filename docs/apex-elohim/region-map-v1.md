# Apex Elohim: region map v1 (draft for approval)

Image: `region-map-v1.png`, drawn over the "fictional Michigan" satellite reference
(`Satellite_map_of_fictional_Michigan_728a143c.jpg` in the racer asset folder).
Nothing here is built yet. This is the layout to approve first.

## Anchor choice
Satellite refs, per Kenne. The coast, river-split port city and farm grid come from the
satellite image. Zone names, circuit roles, Port Halcyon and the festival come from
`ironport-complete/docs/vertical-slice-maps.md` (canon).

## Scale
- About 12 m per pixel gives roughly 16.5 x 9.2 km, about 150 sq km (Horizon-scale).
- Coast to downtown is about 13 km, which satisfies "about 10 miles inland".
- The package canon had an 8 km corridor and about 95 sq km. This draft supersedes it.
  The roster and canon docs still decide building and car numbers.

## What changed from the raw satellite image
1. River fed from the lake. The existing meandering creek is widened and extended west to
   the lake, then runs across the farmland into the harbor, so Ironport is split by water.
2. Kettle Hills moved to the southern ponds. The image has no moraine hills, so that area
   needs new terrain. Canon puts them NE, but NE here is suburb.
3. The farmland grid, coast dunes and downtown are kept as the image shows them.

## Circuits (3)
1. Ironport Grand Circuit: 4.2 km, 16 corners, clockwise, downtown. Canon corner table.
2. Harborfront (festival) circuit: west-bank docks. Based on the Chora festival hub
   circuit ref. Name and layout to be decided.
3. Great Lakes Dune Pass: country raceway on the bluff, partial lake view. Based on the
   Dune Pass route and elevation refs.

Circuit shapes on the image are placeholders for position and size. Real geometry comes
from the circuit sheets plus Terra centerlines.

## Vertical slice
City core of about 3.2 x 2.4 km (white box): Grand Circuit, 86 buildings, Terra parcel
footprints for the downtown blocks. Parcels are real Detroit data. Elsewhere the street
layout is inferred from the satellite fabric.

## Open questions
- The Harborfront circuit is my addition. Canon puts the festival at Port Halcyon, not the city.
- Kettle Hills needs invented terrain. The image has none.
- The freeway is drawn conceptually. The satellite road network decides its real route.
