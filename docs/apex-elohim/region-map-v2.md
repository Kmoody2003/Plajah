# Apex Elohim: region map v2 (draft)

Image: `region-map-v2.png`. Machine-readable circuits, river and roads: `region-v2.json`
(pixel coordinates on the satellite tile, 12 m per pixel).

## Changes from v1
- Three circuits, as Kenne defined them:
  1. Ironport Grand Circuit: city street circuit, waypoints on the real downtown grid.
  2. Pinewood Rally Stage: countryside rally loop (about 10 km). It runs on the existing
     farm gravel roads and forest track. One stretch is a new cut trail through the pines (dashed).
  3. Great Lakes Coast Raceway: permanent road course on the coastal meadow behind the
     dunes, near the lake. Paddock sits on an existing building cluster, with an access
     road to the coastal highway.
- Harborfront circuit removed.
- River mouth moved north, onto the bluffs, so the dune coast is unbroken.
- Coastal highway and freeway re-traced onto the real roads in the satellite image.

## Known gaps
- Grand Circuit length is 5.0 km. The canon says 4.2 km, so it needs tightening or the
  canon needs updating.
- Corner names on the city circuit are approximate placements.
- The raceway shape is hand-drawn to fit the meadow. Elevation and the lake-view
  sightlines need checking against terrain.
- The region is still one satellite tile (about 150 sq km). More tiles come later.
