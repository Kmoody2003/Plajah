Plajah Academia · Classroom releaseOctober 2026

Less time running the class. More time teaching it.

This release focuses on the daily work of operating a classroom: planning the week, handing out and collecting work, grading fairly, supporting every learner and keeping families informed. It works from a teacher's phone as well as a desk.

BEFORE CLASS

Plan and prepare

A full year, already laid out

K–8 Math and ELA in 180 days, aligned to Common Core with international crosswalks. Each day becomes a printable or digital worksheet with its own teacher key.

Worksheets that adapt

Generate easier or harder versions of the same skill for different groups, with 108 original reading passages.

Bring in what you already use

Drop files, links or photos of paper into Tela Classwork. Each one becomes an assignment, and its answer key is paired automatically.

Templates you can share

Save lessons and flyers to a template library. Sharing across the school is off by default, and student and school details are stripped first.

DURING CLASS

Teach and assign

Teacher Tools on your real classes

Pick a class and its real roster loads, with gradebook, planning, reports and assessment on one screen.

Run it from your phone

Teacher and student hubs switch to tabs on a phone, such as Today, Classes, Review and Tools. A slide-out menu opens every tool, and buttons are sized for thumbs.

Paper scanner

Photograph a blank worksheet and the key is built for you and matched to the class in use. Students scan their finished paper to check it and turn it in.

Supports that follow the learner

IEP and 504 plans, or supports with no diagnosis needed. Read-aloud, a reading ruler and break timers switch on in every lesson.

AFTER CLASS

Review and grade

One review queue

Turned-in work lands in a single queue. Approve it with a grade or send it back with notes.

Grading that can be trusted

Answer keys never reach student devices. Work is checked and scored on the server, and students can't change their own scores. Rolling out

No made-up numbers

A student with no evidence yet shows a dash, not an invented score. Sample data appears only on the labelled sample class.

Assessments that save

Recording a student's mastery now saves reliably for the teacher. Before this release, those saves could fail without warning.

ALL WEEK

Keep everyone in the loop

Announcements with living flyers

Post to a class, all members or the public, with polls and surveys built in.

School papers onto the calendar

Scan a flyer or permission slip and its dates go onto student and family calendars, along with worksheet due dates. Rolling out

Meetings for conferences and staff

Schedule a meeting with a guest link for families, then record it with captions and a recap. Rolling out

Quiet hours

Mute a class chat or set quiet hours so messages wait until morning. Mentions still get through. Rolling out
