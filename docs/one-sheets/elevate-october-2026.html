Plajah Elevate · Release highlights

Run the whole organization from one place.

October 2026

Elevate now covers the work an organization does every week: bringing people in, keeping teams talking, leading worship, handling money, finding funding and teaching. It's built for churches, faith communities, nonprofits, charities and cultural institutions.

Onboard everyone at once

For admins and office staff
  • Seats ready before people arrive. Import a member or staff list and every person gets a reserved account.
  • Self-claim. Members claim their seat with a member ID and last name, or their own email. The server checks the match.
  • Join link and QR code for bulletins, screens and signs.
  • Staff from payroll. Import from a file export, Paychex Flex, or any provider through Finch. Pay data is never copied.

Keep teams talking

For staff, departments and volunteers
  • A chat channel for every department, plus its rooms, created automatically and kept in step with who serves where.
  • Public and Members feeds. One switch on the org page; members-only posts are tinted violet and locked. Rolling out
  • Huddles and scheduled meetings with calendar invites and browser guest links that wait in a lobby.
  • Recordings, captions and recaps for meetings, kept on the device by default. Rolling out

Lead worship

For pastors and worship teams
  • Sermon Prep. Text, study, outline, script, then a large-type Preach mode with a timer that keeps the screen awake.
  • Phone Bible that follows the service. The reader turns to each Scripture cue live, in the member's own translation.
  • Clean screens. Profanity is left out of on-screen transcription and lyrics. Operators still see the raw feed.
  • Wording that fits the tradition. Service and prayer labels follow the church's denomination.

Handle the money

For treasurers and finance teams
  • Giving statements as PDFs, emailed in batches to the right household.
  • Gift receipts sent automatically after online gifts.
  • My Giving lets members download their own statements.
  • Restricted-fund guardrail. Spending can't exceed a restricted fund's balance, and releases post as journal entries.
  • Payout account locked. Only a platform admin can change where online gifts are paid out. Rolling out

Find funding

For directors and grant writers
  • Grant Matcher searches Grants.gov and federal open data and ranks results by relevance to your mission.
  • Grant workspace with Aria. A readiness interview, then drafts and PDF prefill from your saved profile.
  • A five-seat review council scores each draft before you submit it.
  • Grant School, a 30-lesson course for first-time applicants.

Teach and form

For Christian Ed and program leaders
  • Class clubs. Each class has a feed, lessons, work, a live room and a people list, joined with a code.
  • Classwork in Tela. Files, links and scanned pages become assignments, and answer keys are paired automatically.
  • Announcements with living flyers, polls and surveys.
  • A shared template library. Sharing is off by default, and org details are stripped from anything shared.
Real organizations see only their own real data.

A new org page starts honestly empty. It shows no sample people, schedules or viewer counts. Grant drafts never invent a fact and never fill a signature or certification. Sample content lives only on the Grace Chapel demo.

Also new: a calmer Vellum look across org, member, staff and volunteer screens, a phone pass with larger tap targets, and a landing page with a tab for each kind of organization. Rolling out means the feature is built and live in the app, and its final permission update is still being deployed.
