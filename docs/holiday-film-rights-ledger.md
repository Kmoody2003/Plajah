# Holiday film rights ledger

Research date: 2026-11-01. This is research, not a legal opinion. Status means the United States only.

Records checked: the US Copyright Office public records search (https://publicrecords.copyright.gov), which holds renewals from 1978 on. Older renewals are only in the printed Catalog of Copyright Entries, which was not searched, so "no renewal found" is weak evidence for any film whose renewal window closed before 1978.

Screen every silent print: the film can be public domain while an added score or new title cards are not.

## Music (US, 2026 cutoffs from `services/publicDomainRights.ts`)

Compositions published through 1930 and recordings through 1925 are free. Both must clear: a free carol in a 1950 recording is not free. Modern arrangements are separate works. On 2027-01-01 the cutoffs move to 1931 and 1926.

Great 78 Project (`collection:78rpm`), dated through 1925, title matches, overlapping: Christmas 125, Holy Night 74, Silent Night 54, Adeste Fideles 40, Noel 33, Hark the Herald 16, Joy to the World 9. Dates are archive metadata, so run each through the rights engine and listen for quality. Not free: White Christmas, Rudolph, Winter Wonderland, and other later songs.

## Before anything goes live

- Geo-limit to the US unless a film is cleared abroad. All of these except the two pre-1931 films rest on US-only rules.
- Have a copyright lawyer or a title search firm confirm any film without a term-expired basis. The printed-catalog check is cheap but does not cover underlying rights.
- Screen each print for added music, new title cards, and distributor logos.
