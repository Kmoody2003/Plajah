Tela Sign is Plajah's own e-signature, built into the same documents people already write, fill and share. It does what you expect from DocuSign, and it was designed around the things people complain about most.

  • ✓No seats, no envelope caps. Signing is included in Plajah plans and in the Civic Rate for governments.
  • ✓Requests people can trust. They arrive in the Plajah app from a verified sender, and emails never ask for a password.
  • ✓Proof you can check yourself. Every step is chained by SHA-256, so changing the history after the fact shows.

Identity checks

The right check for the document, and no more.

Higher-risk documents ask for a stronger check, and the certificate records which one was used. Most of it we build. The one piece we buy is the photo-and-selfie scan, because deepfake defense and biometric privacy law are a full-time job.
