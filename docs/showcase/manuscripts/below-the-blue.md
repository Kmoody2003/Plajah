# Below the Blue
*For ages 6-8  |  Watercolor painting  |  512 words  |  13 pages (spreads) incl. cover and back*

**One line:** A reef fish who thinks the sun has vanished dives into the deep and learns that light can come from inside, and that dark places are never empty.
**Theme:** Curiosity beats fear; light and friendship can come from unexpected places
**Refrain the child can join in on:** "Light can come from inside, too."
**Back-cover blurb:** When a cloud hides the sun, Coral the little reef fish is sure the light is gone for good. She follows a trail of tiny glowing lights down into the deep, and finds out where light really comes from.

## Characters
- **Coral**, round little reef fish (hero). bold, curious, quick to worry, brave when it counts. *Voice:* Quick, excited and a little dramatic; slows down when she is scared. *Signature:* Does a happy somersault when she is delighted. *Arc:* Starts afraid of losing the sun; ends unafraid of the dark and a friend to someone who lives in it.
- **Lumi**, tiny silver lanternfish (companion). calm, gentle, wise in a small way, shy humour. *Voice:* Soft and slow, with a bubbling laugh. *Signature:* Her dots glow brighter when she is happy. *Arc:* Starts as a stranger in the dark; ends as Coral's night-time friend.
- **Mabel**, old humpback whale (supporting). huge, gentle, musical. *Voice:* Hums long, low notes: "hmmmmm". *Signature:* Sings a song that makes the water tremble. *Arc:* Appears at night as the surprise at the end.
- **The jellies**, glowing jellyfish (chorus). sparkly, silent. *Voice:* They say nothing; they glow. *Signature:* Light up in rings when Lumi passes. *Arc:* Show Coral how many kinds of light there are.

## Questions to ask while reading together
1. Why did Coral think the sun was gone? What was really happening?
2. How do animals in the deep sea see without sunlight?
3. What does Lumi mean by "light can come from inside, too"?
4. Have you ever been afraid of the dark? What helped?

## Words to know
- **bioluminescence**: Light made by a living animal's own body, like a firefly or a deep-sea jelly.
- **lanternfish**: A small deep-sea fish with rows of tiny lights along its body.
- **humpback whale**: A very large whale famous for its long, haunting songs.

## Pokee's editor notes (AI second opinion; already acted on where noted in docs/SHOWCASE_BOOKS.md)
- **Structure:** The narrative arc is clear and effective for the target age. The transition from fear to understanding is well-paced. The ending reinforces the theme nicely with the promise to meet at night.
- **Best line (Pokee's pick):** Light can come from inside, too.
- **Suggestion, spread 7 (reword):** The phrase 'It fades out about as deep as a skyscraper is tall' is a bit clunky for read-aloud. Consider simplifying to 'Sunlight fades out before it gets this deep' to keep the rhythm smoother and the concept clearer for 6-year-olds.
- **Suggestion, spread 10 (add):** Mabel's appearance is a bit abrupt. Adding a small line about Coral being surprised or impressed by the whale's song could enhance the emotional connection and make the 'surprise' feel more earned.
