# Orbit Party!
*For ages 4-6  |  Torn cut-paper collage  |  239 words  |  11 pages (spreads) incl. cover and back*

**One line:** A little astronaut learns that the best way to invite a shy friend is to sit beside them, not to push.
**Theme:** Shyness, patience and inviting without pushing
**Refrain the child can join in on:** "Even a planet can feel shy."
**Back-cover blurb:** Zib has invited every planet in the sky to a party. Everyone comes, except Mars. Balloons do not work. Jokes do not work. What does a shy planet need?

## Characters
- **Zib**, round little astronaut (hero). enthusiastic, kind, a bit wobbly, learns to listen. *Voice:* Fast and excited at first, then slow and gentle when it matters. *Signature:* Blows a kazoo: "Toot-toot!" And his knees go wobbly when he is shy. *Arc:* Starts out pushing; learns to sit still and share a feeling; ends with a friend who comes out in their own time.
- **Nova**, comet pup (companion). playful, loyal, sniffy. *Voice:* Woofs and sniffs: "Sniff! Sniff! Woof!" *Signature:* Zips in a circle and leaves a trail of sparkles. *Arc:* Sniffs out the guests; is the first to sit still beside Mars.
- **Mars**, shy red planet (supporting). shy, gentle, secretly funny. *Voice:* A whisper: "Too loud. Too many." *Signature:* Hides half behind a crater, and blushes. *Arc:* Hides; is invited gently; comes out slowly and dances small.
- **Saturn**, ringed planet guest (chorus). twirly, show-off. *Voice:* Whoooo-oo! *Signature:* Spins his ring. *Arc:* Arrives with a twirl; cheers for Mars.
- **Jupiter**, giant striped planet guest (chorus). booming, friendly. *Voice:* "HO HO HO!" (but he whispers it for Mars). *Signature:* Laughs so big the stars shake. *Arc:* Learns to whisper.

## Questions to ask while reading together
1. Why did Mars hide? How could you tell Mars was shy?
2. What did Zib try first? Did it work?
3. What worked in the end? Why do you think that helped?
4. What do you do when you feel shy? What helps you?

## Pokee's editor notes (AI second opinion; already acted on where noted in docs/SHOWCASE_BOOKS.md)
- **Structure:** The story follows a clear emotional arc from 'pushing' to 'listening.' The turning point is distinct and effectively resolves the conflict through empathy rather than action.
- **Best line (Pokee's pick):** Even a planet can feel shy.
- **Suggestion, spread 6 (reword):** The joke 'Moon-jamas' is a bit long and complex for a 4-year-old to process in the middle of a fast-paced sequence of failed attempts. It might break the rhythm. Consider a simpler, more visual failure (e.g., 'Zib tried a big hug. Mars flinched.').
- **Suggestion, spread 9 (reword):** The line 'Even a shy planet can find the beat' is slightly abstract. For ages 4-6, concrete actions are better. Consider: 'Even a shy planet can dance small.'

## Remix ideas
- Make the shy guest a different planet, or a different animal, or a classmate.
- Change the party: a picnic, a sleepover, a birthday.
- Write what YOU do when you feel shy and swap it in for Zib's knees.
- Draw your own planets and paste them over the paper cut-outs.
