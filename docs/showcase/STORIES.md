# Moon Blanket
*For ages 2-4  |  Felt and stitched textile  |  118 words  |  11 pages (spreads) incl. cover and back*

**One line:** A little bear finds a hole in his blanket and discovers a cold moth inside, so he shares his warmth.
**Theme:** Sharing what you have, and making room for someone small
**Refrain the child can join in on:** "Snug as a stitch."
**Back-cover blurb:** Bramble the bear has a big, soft, moon-colored blanket. Tonight it has a hole. Who made it? A small, shivery someone who needs a snug place to sleep.

## Characters
- **Bramble**, small honey-brown bear (hero). gentle, sleepy, curious, kind. *Voice:* Speaks softly and slowly, in very short sentences. *Signature:* Pulls the blanket up to his chin and says "Snug as a stitch." *Arc:* Starts puzzled by the hole in his blanket; ends happy to share it.
- **Flit**, tiny moth (companion). shy, shivery, grateful. *Voice:* A whisper-small voice that trembles at first, then gets warmer. *Signature:* Flutters twice before she speaks: flutter, flutter. *Arc:* Starts cold and hidden; ends warm and safe inside the blanket.
- **The Moon**, big round felt moon (supporting). calm, watchful, kind. *Voice:* Never speaks; only smiles. *Signature:* Its smile grows a little on every page. *Arc:* Watches over the room; at the end it is the last thing the reader says goodnight to.

- **Structure:** The narrative arc is tight and perfectly suited for a picture book. The transition from 'puzzled' to 'kind' is clear and immediate, which is appropriate for the 2-4 age group. The 'hole' serves as both a plot device and a thematic symbol for making room.

# Beep Block Street
*For ages 3-5  |  Vibrant pop and screen-print graphics  |  173 words  |  10 pages (spreads) incl. cover and back*

**One line:** A bus loses his beep and the whole street gets stuck, until a small pigeon finds it in an unexpected place.
**Theme:** Small friends can fix big problems, and a voice matters most when it is missing
**Refrain the child can join in on:** "Beep!"
**Back-cover blurb:** Every morning, Bo the bus wakes up Beep Block Street with one great big BEEP. Then one grey morning, the beep is gone. Who can find it? Maybe the smallest friend on the street.

## Characters
- **Bo**, square red bus (hero). cheerful, loud, loyal, a little proud. *Voice:* Big, bold and bouncy. Says everything with a beep. *Signature:* A great big BEEP! to start the day, and a small beep to say goodnight. *Arc:* Starts proud of his loud beep; loses it; learns to say thank you to the small friend who found it.
- **Pip**, small city pigeon (companion). curious, brave, tiny but determined. *Voice:* Quick little coos: "Coo? Coo!" *Signature:* Tilts his head to one side when he is thinking. *Arc:* Starts as a pigeon who naps on Bo's roof; becomes the hero who finds the beep.
- **Tilly the Taxi**, yellow taxi (chorus). impatient, honky, softens in the end. *Voice:* Honks when she is cross. *Signature:* HONK! *Arc:* Cross at first; waves hello at the end.
- **Gus the Truck**, big green dump truck (chorus). grumbly, big, soft-hearted. *Voice:* A deep "Toot-toot!" that is much smaller than he is. *Signature:* TOOT! *Arc:* Stuck and grumpy at first; cheers loudest at the end.

# Orbit Party!
*For ages 4-6  |  Torn cut-paper collage  |  239 words  |  11 pages (spreads) incl. cover and back*

**One line:** A little astronaut learns that the best way to invite a shy friend is to sit beside them, not to push.
**Theme:** Shyness, patience and inviting without pushing
**Refrain the child can join in on:** "Even a planet can feel shy."
**Back-cover blurb:** Zib has invited every planet in the sky to a party. Everyone comes, except Mars. Balloons do not work. Jokes do not work. What does a shy planet need?

## Characters
- **Zib**, round little astronaut (hero). enthusiastic, kind, a bit wobbly, learns to listen. *Voice:* Fast and excited at first, then slow and gentle when it matters. *Signature:* Blows a kazoo: "Toot-toot!" And his knees go wobbly when he is shy. *Arc:* Starts out pushing; learns to sit still and share a feeling; ends with a friend who comes out in their own time.
- **Nova**, comet pup (companion). playful, loyal, sniffy. *Voice:* Woofs and sniffs: "Sniff! Sniff! Woof!" *Signature:* Zips in a circle and leaves a trail of sparkles. *Arc:* Sniffs out the guests; is the first to sit still beside Mars.
- **Mars**, shy red planet (supporting). shy, gentle, secretly funny. *Voice:* A whisper: "Too loud. Too many." *Signature:* Hides half behind a crater, and blushes. *Arc:* Hides; is invited gently; comes out slowly and dances small.
- **Saturn**, ringed planet guest (chorus). twirly, show-off. *Voice:* Whoooo-oo! *Signature:* Spins his ring. *Arc:* Arrives with a twirl; cheers for Mars.
- **Jupiter**, giant striped planet guest (chorus). booming, friendly. *Voice:* "HO HO HO!" (but he whispers it for Mars). *Signature:* Laughs so big the stars shake. *Arc:* Learns to whisper.

# Little Fox, Big Trees
*For ages 5-7  |  Linocut / woodblock print  |  340 words  |  11 pages (spreads) incl. cover and back*

**One line:** A small fox who is afraid of the towering forest finds the courage to help a lost fawn, and the trees turn out to be gentle.
**Theme:** Courage is helping someone else while you are still scared
**Refrain the child can join in on:** "Left foot, right foot, hush."
**Back-cover blurb:** Tam is a little fox, and tonight Tam must walk home through the biggest trees in the world, alone. Then something small steps out from behind a trunk, and it needs Tam's help.

## Characters
- **Tam**, little fox (hero). careful, brave in small steps, kind, honest about being scared. *Voice:* Whispers when scared, talks plainly when brave. *Signature:* Counts their steps out loud: "Left foot, right foot, hush." *Arc:* Starts afraid of being small; ends knowing small is no reason not to help.
- **Dot**, spotted fawn (companion). lost, polite, surprisingly funny, trusting. *Voice:* Soft and polite: "Hello. I am small too." *Signature:* Says "I am small too" and blinks slowly. *Arc:* Starts lost; ends reunited with her family and a new friend.
- **Mama Fox**, grown fox (supporting). warm, steady, proud. *Voice:* Calm, short sentences. *Signature:* Leaves a lantern of fireflies at the den door. *Arc:* Waits at the den; understands without being told.
- **The Big Trees**, towering trunks (chorus). silent, watchful, gentle. *Voice:* Never speak. They hold the light. *Signature:* A gold, sleepy light in every trunk at sunset. *Arc:* Look frightening; end up looking like kind giants keeping watch.
