## Contradicted or outdated (confirmed, fixed)

1. l14 splenic embolization vaccination (lesson, l14.q5, new guideline anchor). Was: "After splenectomy or splenic embolization, vaccinate". Now: vaccines after splenectomy; after angioembolization with the spleen in place, EAST 2022 conditionally recommends against routine post-splenectomy vaccines (immune function similar to controls, level III evidence). l14.q5 was rewritten around the guideline: the correct option is "No routine post-splenectomy vaccine series is required", and the old "no immunization" distractor is gone. Source opened: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36038390/ (Injury 2022;53:3569). Secondary: https://thetraumapro.com/2023/08/25/new-east-practice-guideline-spleen-vaccines-after-angioembolization/
2. l18 adrenal incidentaloma "above about 4 cm is resected". Now teaches ESE/ENSAT 2023: dedicated imaging, metanephrines and a 1 mg dexamethasone test; a homogeneous mass of 10 HU or less is benign regardless of size; other masses go to a multidisciplinary meeting; surgery is usual for masses over 4 cm that are inhomogeneous or over 20 HU; mild autonomous cortisol secretion (post-dexamethasone cortisol above 1.8 ug/dL) is individualized with comorbidities. Guideline anchor added. l18.q2 stem changed from "operative feature" to "histologic finding in the resected specimen" (verifier B wording note). Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37318239/ (Eur J Endocrinol 2023;189:G1).
3. l16 Parkland formula. Was: 4 mL/kg/%TBSA presented as current. Now: original Parkland (Baxter and Shires, 1968) is 4 mL, modified Brooke is 2 mL, and the 2024 ABA guideline (adults, 20% TBSA or more) recommends starting at 2 mL/kg/%TBSA then titrating to urine output. l16.q2 explanation updated; ABA 2024 anchor added. I could not open an ATLS text, so ATLS is not cited for the 2 mL figure. Sources opened: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38051821/ (J Burn Care Res 2024;45:565); history of formulas at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12285936/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9754771/
4. l13 ATLS edition. Anchor changed from 10th edition 2018 to 11th edition 2025; lesson now says ATLS 11 (2025) adds "x" (exsanguinating external hemorrhage), giving xABCDE. Sources: https://www.facs.org/for-medical-professionals/news-publications/news-and-articles/acs-brief/september-16-2025-issue/trauma-care-gets-major-upgrade-with-launch-of-atls-11/ and https://www.facs.org/quality-programs/trauma/education/advanced-trauma-life-support/atls-11/
5. l20 prophylaxis timing blended guidelines. Lesson now states each separately: ASHP/IDSA/SIS/SHEA 2013 within 60 minutes before incision (120 for vancomycin and fluoroquinolones), stop within 24 hours; CDC 2017 gives no fixed minutes, asks for bactericidal levels at incision and no extra doses after closure in clean and clean-contaminated cases; WHO 2016 within 120 minutes with the drug half-life in mind. l20.q2 now asks specifically about the ASHP-led 2013 guideline. Sources opened: https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/policy-guidelines/docs/therapeutic-guidelines/therapeutic-guidelines-antimicrobial-prophylaxis-surgery.pdf ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28467526/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8743832/ (states WHO 120 minutes); WHO evidence appendix https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/integrated-health-services-(ihs)/ssi/evidence/appendix5.pdf
