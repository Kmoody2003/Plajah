# med-premed-behavioral: claims for expert review

- l01: Glutamate/GABA, dopamine/serotonin role summaries are simplified; "prefrontal cortex matures into the mid-twenties" is a common approximation.
- l02: Smell reaching olfactory cortex without a thalamic relay (textbook, but nuance about thalamic involvement later). Gate control theory stated as "proposes", not settled.
- l03: Ventral/dorsal stream "what/where-how" framing is the common simplification; contested in detail.
- l04: Mirror neurons' role in humans stated as debated. Garcia effect attribution wording ("Garcia effect work") is loose. Little Albert described as ethically unacceptable (consensus view).
- l05: "Seven plus or minus two" working memory capacity (modern estimates are lower, about four chunks). NMDA dependence of LTP stated generally. Korsakoff confabulation presented as typical, not universal.
- l06: Spearman g, Gardner and Sternberg summaries; Piaget is mentioned briefly here, detailed in l11.
- l07: Developmental timeline ages (cooing about 2 months, babbling about 6, first words about 12, two-word about 18 to 24 months) are approximate. Weak linguistic relativity color-naming evidence is simplified. Conduction aphasia attributed to arcuate fasciculus (classic model, debated).
- l08: Ekman six basic emotions cross-cultural claim is contested. LeDoux fast amygdala route is a classic simplification. Schachter-Singer used epinephrine; its replicability is disputed. Yerkes-Dodson complex-task claim.
- l09: Strict ordering of Maslow needs lacks cross-cultural support (stated). Lateral/ventromedial hypothalamus model of feeding is the textbook model, now considered simplified. Masters and Johnson cycle named as classic model.
- l10: Heritability "around 40 to 50 percent" for personality traits is an approximate range. Defense mechanism list standard. Rorschach/TAT validity said to be debated.
- l11: Piaget age ranges approximate and later research suggests earlier competence. Kubler-Ross stages stated as non-universal. Erikson stage names standard.
- l12: DSM-5-TR (2022) named as the current DSM as of this writing. Dopamine hypothesis described as incomplete. "Most people with a mental disorder are not violent" is standard consensus.
- l13: Asch and Milgram summaries stated qualitatively without percentages. The $1 versus $20 dissonance study (Festinger and Carlsmith) described from memory. Zimbardo criticism noted generally.
- l14: Kitty Genovese press account said to be corrected by later reporting. Stereotype threat effect size said to be questioned. Implicit bias predictive validity noted as debated. Pygmalion effect evidence described as "in some studies".
- l15: Attribution of theories to Durkheim, Parsons, Marx, Weber, Mead, Blumer, Bourdieu (standard). Gini coefficient scale 0 to 1. Statement that there is no valid biological subdivision of humans into discrete races (scientific consensus).
- l16: Whitehall study described qualitatively. Tuskegee study mention (accepted history). Intersectionality attributed to Crenshaw. Q on maternal complications relies on the well-documented disparity, no numbers given.
- l17: Transtheoretical model stage durations not stated; model has its own critics. Placebo stronger for subjective outcomes (consensus).
- l18: Definition of p-value paraphrased; Belmont Report principles listed correctly to my knowledge. Preregistration said to be "increasingly encouraged".
