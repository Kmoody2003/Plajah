# med-ethics-young: items for expert review

- l05: "for children the law says a parent or guardian usually gives permission, child gives assent". Correct as a general US/Western framing; exceptions (mature minor, emergency, minor-consent laws) vary by state and country and are not taught.
- l05: "some health care is needed ... a grown-up decides": simplified; does not cover parental refusals or courts.
- l06: confidentiality exception (danger to self or others, suspected abuse) is simplified; mandated-reporter rules vary by place and profession.
- l09: "licensed, finish training and pass exams" and "government agencies check medicines before sale" are simplified for a general audience (US FDA-style framing).
- l10: trial "steps" (small safety study first, then larger efficacy studies) is a simplification of Phase I to III.
- l11: four principles are Beauchamp and Childress (named only as "four ideas"); the Grandpa story is an invented illustration.
- l12: "in many places some teen conversations with a doctor can be private": varies widely by jurisdiction and age.
- l12: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (US call or text). Verify the service name and number are current.
