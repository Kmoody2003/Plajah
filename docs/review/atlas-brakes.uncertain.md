# atlas-brakes-g68 / atlas-brakes-hs / atlas-brakes-college: items for expert review

All three courses are Draft and safety-critical: need review by an ASE-certified master technician before any verified label. Nothing here has been independently verified.

## Numbers and standards to verify
- hs l09: FMVSS 116 minimum boiling points quoted as approximate (DOT 3 about 205 C dry / 140 C wet; DOT 4 about 230 / 155; DOT 5.1 about 260 / 180). Check current standard text; the lesson flags them as commonly cited minimums. DOT 5 silicone "not mixable" and "generally not recommended with ABS" (manual-dependent). Statement that glycol grades 3/4/5.1 "can generally be mixed".
- hs l11: ESC required on new US passenger vehicles "from about the 2012 model year" (FMVSS 126 phase-in); lesson says verify.
- hs l10: bleeding order "often farthest from the master cylinder first" (varies); warning against full pedal stroke on old master cylinders (corroded bore damages seals).
- hs l01 / college l01: pedal ratio 4:1 to 5:1, booster ratio 3 to 4, bore sizes are illustrative worked-example values, not specifications.
- hs l06: pad family descriptions (organic / semi-metallic / ceramic) are qualitative generalizations.
- hs l07: "several evenly spaced points" for thickness-variation measurement; rotor machining allowance example (0.15 mm per face) is illustrative.
- g68 l10: "about three seconds" following gap is a commonly taught figure; defers to the local driver handbook.
- g68 l10 / college l04: doubling speed roughly quadruples braking distance (physics: d = v^2/2a); dry-asphalt deceleration range 0.7 to 1.0 g is a commonly cited range.
- college l04/l05: all arithmetic recomputed by the author: KE 578.8 kJ; d = 49.2 m at 0.8 g; 196.7 m at double speed; 1.5 s reaction 41.7 m; 164 kW average; rotor delta T about 57 K using c = 460 J/kg K for cast iron (specific heat value to verify); 4.41 MJ descent, 432 K no-cooling bound.
- college l03: ideal front share 71.9% at 0.8 g and 63.7% at 0.4 g for the illustrative vehicle (m 1,500 kg, L 2.70 m, b 1.50 m, h 0.55 m).
- college l06: peak-slip range "10 to 30 percent" and dry/wet/snow peak mu ranges are rough commonly cited values; "ABS can lengthen stopping distance on loose gravel / deep snow" is a standard but surface-dependent claim.
- college l07: select-low rear control and individual front control are typical, not universal; valve state table (apply/hold/release) is the common 3-state description.
- college l08: passive vs active sensor behavior; tone ring frequency example (48 teeth, 2.0 m circumference, 20 m/s gives 480 Hz; 33.3 Hz at 1.389 m/s); turn example outer 10.15 / inner 9.85 m/s.
- college l09: pressure-bleed "roughly 10 to 20 psi" (follow equipment/vehicle instructions); isothermal bubble model V = V0 P0/P.
- college l10: regeneration is conceptual; the 60 percent recovery fraction is an illustration, not a measured figure; HV safety statements are general and defer to OEM procedure.
- college l11: EPB service-mode / calibration statements are generic; ADAS calibration after windshield work is OEM-dependent.
- college l13: Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. 2301 et seq.) summary on tie-in provisions; FMVSS 135 described as the light vehicle brake performance standard. Not legal advice; verify with counsel. Record-retention, estimate and licensing rules vary by state.
- college l12: pulsation frequency example (13.9 Hz at 27.78 m/s, 2.0 m circumference); mineral-oil contamination swelling rubber seals.
