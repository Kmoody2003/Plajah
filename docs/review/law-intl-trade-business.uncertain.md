# law-intl-trade-business: claims for expert review

- l01: Marrakesh Agreement in force 1 Jan 1995; GATT Article II/III/XI characterisations; "like products" factors described as historical practice, with regulatory-purpose approach in "some reports" (contested).
- l02: Article 31bis amendment in force January 2017; 2003 waiver; "first-ever amendment to a WTO agreement" (believed correct). Article XVI "six kinds" of prohibited limitations. The 2022 ministerial decision on COVID vaccines is referred to only in an answer explanation.
- l03: Appellate Body paralysis dates (only one member left 11 Dec 2019; last term ended Nov 2020); statement that the US objected to continued decisions past 90 days and precedent treatment; MPIA participants "EU, China, Canada, Australia and others" and that the US and India are not members; status "as of this writing" the body is not restored.
- l04: Article XX(g) and (b) outcomes in Shrimp (1998), the 2001 compliance report, Asbestos (2001) treatment of health risk in likeness; Russia - Traffic in Transit (WT/DS512, April 2019); "reports circulated in December 2022" on US steel and aluminium tariffs, and that the US "appealed into the void".
- l05: Turkey - Textiles (AB 1999) reasoning; USMCA in force 1 July 2020; USMCA joint review scheduled 2026 and its outcome (uncertain as of this writing); Article XXIV:5/XXIV:6 characterisations.
- l06: HS revised "about every five years", in force 1988; General Rules of Interpretation summary (order of rules, "last in numerical order"); Customs Valuation Agreement fallback order; section 592 penalty tiers (fraud, gross negligence, negligence); description of sections 232 and 301 (no IEEPA tariff litigation is covered, deliberately).
- l07: TBT Articles 2.1, 2.2, 2.4 summaries; Clove Cigarettes (AB 2012) holding on likeness and legitimate regulatory distinction; EC-Hormones (AB 1998) outcome and its risk-assessment reasoning; SPS Article 5.7 summary.
- l08: Anti-dumping: sunset review five years; the claim that zeroing has been "repeatedly" found inconsistent and "remains contested"; that the US does not apply the lesser duty rule; SCM specificity and prohibited-subsidy categories; Safeguards: four years extendable to eight, and retaliation right "after three years in some cases"; US Title VII roles of Commerce and ITC.
- l09: CISG in force 1 Jan 1988; list of Contracting States and that the UK and India are not parties (check current status); US/China Article 95 reservation (China's reservation status believed correct, verify); Article summaries (11, 14, 16, 18(2), 19); MCC-Marble (11th Cir. 1998) holding on the parol evidence rule.
- l10: Article 25, 35, 38, 39(2), 46, 47, 49, 50, 74, 77, 78, 79 summaries; Delchi v. Rotorex (2d Cir. 1995) holding on fundamental breach and lost profits; Incoterms 2020: eleven rules, names (DPU), CIF minimum insurance, risk passing on loading under CIF and FOB.
- l11: Prima Paint (1967) and Buckeye (2006) as separability authorities; Model Law Article 16 reference; ICC scrutiny of draft awards; First Options (1995) holding; claim that emergency arbitrator provisions are common in modern rules.
- l12: New York Convention in force 1959 and "more than 170 parties"; US made both reciprocity and commercial reservations; Article V(1)(a)-(e), V(2) summary; Scherk (1974) and Mitsubishi (1985) holdings as summarised; Article VI security reference.
- l13: ICSID Convention Articles 25, 52, 53, 54, 55 summaries; Metalclad (2000) facts as summarised (permit denial after assurances); Methanex (2005) reasoning including the "specific commitments" caveat; description of standards of treatment.
- l14: Achmea (CJEU 2018) and Komstroy (2021) holdings; the 2020 agreement to terminate intra-EU BITs; claim that some tribunals and non-EU courts do not accept the EU-law objection; the statement that the EU itself has moved to withdraw from the Energy Charter Treaty (verify status); Philip Morris Asia v. Australia (2015) abuse-of-process reasoning; USMCA ISDS arrangements; ICSID Rules amended with effect from July 2022; UNCITRAL Working Group III reform status.
- l15: BNP Paribas 2014 guilty plea and "about 8.9 billion dollars"; OFAC 50 percent rule; EU Blocking Regulation scope (listed US measures on Cuba and Iran); foreign direct product rule description; presumption of denial for Entity List parties is stated generically (policy varies by entry); Wassenaar described as non-binding.
- l16: FCPA structure, defences and the claim that enforcement priorities shifted with a pause and revised guidelines in 2025 (verify current policy); UK Bribery Act section 7 and the adequate procedures defence; CFIUS legislative history (Exon-Florio 1988, FINSA 2007, FIRRMA 2018); EU 2019 screening regulation as a cooperation mechanism; UK NSIA 2021.
