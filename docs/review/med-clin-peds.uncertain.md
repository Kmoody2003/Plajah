# med-clin-peds: claims for expert review

- l01: CDC charts from 2 years and WHO standards birth to 2 years; AAP 2020 report timing of general screening (9, 18, 30 months) and autism screening (18, 24 months); approximate milestone ages.
- l02: AAP 2022 hyperbilirubinemia guideline described in general terms only (hour-specific thresholds); pulse oximetry fail threshold (below about 90 percent) and 24-hour timing; "up to roughly 10 percent weight loss, regained by about two weeks".
- l03: Antenatal steroid benefits (IVH, NEC) mentioned in an explanation; AAP 2019 GBS report cited; Erb palsy posture description.
- l04: Percentage of Down syndrome from Robertsonian translocation (about 4 percent); AAP 2022 Down syndrome health supervision citation; Turner cardiac/renal associations; 22q11 live-vaccine caveat.
- l05: Sweat chloride cutoff 60 mmol/L; list of newborn screening conditions varies by state; galactosemia E. coli sepsis association; urea cycle presentation details.
- l06: CMV sensorineural hearing loss as main sequela; Zika and parvovirus statements; Ortolani/Barlow and ultrasound age cutoffs (4 to 6 months).
- l07: Immunization schedule ages given in general terms. The US federal (ACIP/CDC) schedule has been under active revision and professional bodies (AAP) have issued divergent schedules; exact current recommendations (for example hepatitis B birth dose, COVID-19, combination vaccines) must be verified against the live schedules. The anchor is labelled 2025. Measles herd immunity threshold of about 95 percent. 1998 retracted autism paper. Egg allergy and influenza vaccine statement.
- l08: AAP 2021 febrile infant guideline summarised loosely (8 to 60 days); Kocher criteria components; dexamethasone in bacterial meningitis (evidence strongest for H. influenzae type b); imaging after first febrile UTI (AAP 2011 UTI guidance; later reappraisals exist).
- l09: AAP 2013 AOM guideline observation criteria; vitamin A in measles; IDSA 2012 backup culture in children; cited as current though newer updates may exist.
- l10: Bronchiolitis monoclonal antibodies (nirsevimab; clesrovimab approval date) and maternal RSV vaccination timing are recent and changing; asthma guideline citation (NHLBI 2007 with 2020 focused update, GINA) and the statement about combined anti-inflammatory reliever approaches by age group.
- l11: Meckel diverticulum "rule of 2s" content; ultrasound-first imaging for appendicitis; ORS use in moderate dehydration; Shiga toxin and antibiotics statement.
- l12: Kawasaki criteria (AHA 2017) and IVIG window of 10 days; rheumatic fever Jones criteria listed only by major manifestations (2015 revision not detailed).
- l13: ISPAD 2022 citation; cerebral oedema management statement; puberty age cutoffs (8 in girls, 9 in boys; delayed at 13 and 14).
- l14: AAP 2011 febrile seizure guideline (lumbar puncture statement under 12 months is simplified); first-line infantile spasm treatments.
- l15: Hydroxyurea and emicizumab statements; neuroblastoma and Wilms associations; screening anaemia at about 12 months.
- l16: TEN-4-FACESp rule paraphrase; skeletal survey age (younger than 2 years); that neglect is the most common maltreatment type; CAPTA description.
- l17: Leading causes of adolescent death differ by data source (firearms now lead in some US data); USPSTF 2022 depression screening; cervical screening starting at 21; chlamydia/gonorrhoea screening under 25.
- l18: AAP 2019 ADHD guideline age bands; autism screening ages; enuresis alarm as best long-term therapy.
