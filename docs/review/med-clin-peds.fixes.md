# med-clin-peds fixes (asOf 2026-10)

## Changed
1. l08 febrile infant (t3.ts body, q2 stem/hint/explanation). Was: all infants <=28 days get full workup and admission. Now: AAP 2021 - 8-21 days full workup and admission; 22-28 days stratified by urinalysis and inflammatory markers (normal markers: LP and antibiotics optional, hospital observation or, after normal CSF, home with reliable 24 h follow-up); <8 days and ill-appearing outside the algorithm. q2 stem changed from 3-week-old to 12-day-old so the keyed answer stays unambiguous. Sources: https://www.aliem.com/the-febrile-infant/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11259401 (AAP site 403; PICO/PDF copy also 403).
2. l14 febrile seizure LP (t5.ts). Was: LP considered "under 12 months". Now: LP if meningeal signs; option at 6-12 months only if Hib/pneumococcal immunization deficient or unknown, or antibiotic pretreatment; age alone is the 1996 rule. Source: https://www.contemporarypediatrics.com/view/revised-aap-guidelines-address-simple-febrile-seizures-children
3. l07 immunization (t3.ts body + anchor). Was: "core principles stable" list with no dated status. Now dated, flagged fast-moving. AAP 2026 schedule (26 Jan 2026, 12 endorsing organisations) keeps 18 diseases routine. Court (AAP v. Kennedy, Judge Murphy) stayed the memo, new ACIP appointments and votes on 16 Mar 2026, restoring the June 2024 schedule; HHS appealed; First Circuit argument scheduled 6 Oct 2026; district proceedings paused 11 Sep 2026. So verifier B is right on legal status and A is right on content of the January memo; they are compatible.
