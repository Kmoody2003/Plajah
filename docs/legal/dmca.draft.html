Plajah Copyright & DMCA Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

Plajah hosts work that its users upload. We respect copyright and respond to notices of claimed infringement under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

Our designated agent

Plajah Copyright Agent · copyright@plajah.com. You can also file through the form in the Plajah Help Center under DMCA & Copyright › File a notice. The form checks that a notice has everything the law requires before you send it.

Reporting infringement (takedown notice)

A notice must include all of the following:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature, as the copyright owner or a person authorized to act for the owner.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed (or a representative list, if there are several).
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and where it is on Plajah. A link to each item is best.
  4. Your contact information: name, mailing address, telephone number and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

If a notice is incomplete, we tell you what is missing. When we act on a valid notice we remove or disable access to the material, tell the account that posted it, and send it a copy of the notice details it needs to respond. Your contact details may be shared with that account. Before filing, consider whether the use may be fair use. Knowingly misrepresenting that material is infringing can make you liable for damages under § 512(f).

Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, you can file a counter-notice. Sign in to the account that posted it and use the form in the Help Center, or email the agent. A counter-notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that was removed and where it appeared before removal.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled because of a mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, mailing address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district of your address (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which Plajah may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice or their agent.

We send a copy of a valid counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. We restore the material no sooner than 10 and no later than 14 business days after we receive the counter-notice, unless our agent first receives notice that the original sender has filed a court action seeking to restrain the infringement.

Repeat infringers

Each valid notice we uphold against an account is one strike, however many items it covers. Strikes count for twelve months. An account with three active strikes loses the ability to upload and publish, and we may close it. A strike is removed if the notice is withdrawn or the material is restored after a counter-notice. Account holders can appeal through the account-standing banner.

Not copyright?

Disputes about credits, splits or a licence between collaborators are not DMCA matters. Plajah keeps the record of credits, splits and contributions but is not a party to agreements between creators. Use the “License dispute” option in the form, or email legal@plajah.com.
