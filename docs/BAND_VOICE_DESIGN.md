# Chora Session Band — ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

One prompt per session player (the 13 in `services/melos/beats/band/roster.ts`). Each is built
from the player's character sheet (`band/character/sheets.ts`): pronouns, age, home, timbre,
pace (`rate`), warmth/humor/directness and their own phrases.

**How to use (ElevenLabs → Voices → Voice Design):**
- Paste **Description** into the voice description box (each is under 1,000 characters).
- Paste **Preview text** as the text to speak. It's written in the player's own words, so you
  hear the personality, not just the timbre.
- Generate a few candidates, keep the one that fits, and save it as `Chora · <Name>`.
- If an accent comes out too strong or too weak, change the strength word in the description
  ("light", "gentle", "clear"). That's the lever Voice Design responds to most.

On-device playback still uses each sheet's Kokoro voice. These prompts are for casting higher
quality voices (promos, lessons, recorded talkback).

## Marcus "Deacon" Bell — drums · Memphis, Tennessee · he/him · early fifties

**Description**
A man in his early fifties from Memphis, Tennessee: a veteran session and arena-tour drummer who
grew up playing in church. Low, warm baritone with a smile in it and a light Memphis Southern
accent, with a touch of gravel from years of loud stages. Relaxed, unhurried pace; short phrases
with natural pauses, as if he's leaning back on a drum stool. Generous and encouraging, never
preachy, with a soft chuckle under his words. Studio-quality, close-mic'd, dry recording, no
music or background noise.

**Preview text**
> Alright, count it with me. One, two, one-two-three-four. See, that's it. Sit in the pocket and
> let the snare come to you. Those little ghost notes in between? That's the conversation. Say
> less, play more. Let the church say amen.

## Amara Okafor — percussion · Lagos, Nigeria · Havana, Cuba · she/her · late thirties

**Description**
A woman in her late thirties, a master percussionist raised in Lagos who studied for years in
Havana. Warm, low alto voice, calm and musical, with a teacher's patience. Clear Nigerian English
accent, with natural Cuban Spanish when she slips into a word like "¡Dale!". Medium pace, almost

**Preview text**
