Plajah Privacy & Data Protection

Effective date: 2026-05-17

Overview

Plajah collects and processes personal data necessary to provide the service, including account information, uploaded content metadata, usage metrics, and billing details. We respect data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

Legal Bases

We rely on the following legal bases for processing:

  • Contractual necessity (to provide the Service and fulfill subscriptions).
  • Consent where required for optional features (e.g., marketing communications).
  • Legitimate interests for platform security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

User Rights (EU/UK)

EU and UK users may exercise rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. To exercise rights, contact privacy@plajah.com. We will respond within statutory timeframes.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including transaction history and legal obligations. Users may request deletion; account deletions remove profile data and uploaded content subject to backups and legal hold exceptions.

International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other jurisdictions. Where applicable, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and analytics to operate the Service and for product improvement. Users may manage cookie preferences via their browser or account settings.

Contact & Supervisory Authorities

Data protection questions or complaints can be directed to privacy@plajah.com. EU/UK data subjects may also contact their local supervisory authority.

Updates

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
