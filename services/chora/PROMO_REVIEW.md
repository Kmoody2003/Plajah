# Chora collection review

Six proposed directions are native Tela objects in all four formats. They are review-only; the production selector and default ZIP retain the three original suites. Explicit scene creation and editable board generation support all nine.

The art, typography, motion, accessibility and production council lenses below are a design review method, not a claim that an authenticated Aria council service ran.

| Direction | Art and typography | Motion intention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial Nocturne | Ivory stock, vermilion issue index, serif headlines and generous margins. Editorial listening culture. | Restrained camera breath; keep issue index and CTA readable. |
| Botanical Reverie | Pressed leaves on forest green, warm light and jade annotations. Organic ambient releases. | Slow breathing artwork; quiet botanical atmosphere. |
| Brutalist Signal | Acid yellow, industrial bars, hard alignment and heavyweight type. Club and experimental energy. | Measured pulse with a stable conversion area; no strobing. |
| Celestial Atlas | Gold orbital charts, midnight ground and serif astronomical annotations. Expansive cinematic music. | Gradual drift, steady readable title and QR. |
| Chrome Current | Cobalt fields, reflective current paths and a capsule label. Electronic futurism. | Fluid camera breath, retaining strong contrast. |
| Paper Salon | Rose stock, folded geometric paper and italic serif type. Personal acoustic listening. | Soft breathing movement and a calm end slate. |

Every composition has editable title, artist, artwork slot, campaign line, vector ornaments, wordmarks and a real destination QR. Shared conversion panels retain dark/light contrast. Motion export currently uses the established canvas camera-breath treatment with release audio snippets, rather than per-layer native keyframes. Static Tela boards preserve editability; they do not claim to embed a video timeline.

Audit correction: the motion exporter previously froze only the bottom 16% of each image. That missed the QR/CTA on wide and square formats. It now derives the protected area from the actual conversion divider; the end slate respects that boundary too. Camera scale stays at or above 1 to avoid uncovered edges.

Automated coverage exercises all 36 suite/format combinations, editable document copies, QR paths and destinations, review isolation, audio clamps and unsafe URL rejection. Actual video encoding, playback, remote media loading and authenticated publication require browser integration checks.
