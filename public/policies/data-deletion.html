Delete Your Plajah Data

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Delete your account

  1. Sign in at plajah.com.
  2. Open the delete-account page: plajah.com/delete-account (also under Help Center → Delete my account).
  3. Read what will be removed, confirm, and delete. Your profile, uploaded content and stored settings are removed, subject to backups and legal-hold exceptions described in our Privacy Policy.

Remove only your connected social accounts

Open Marketing → choose the account → Disconnect. This deletes the Facebook, Instagram, X or LinkedIn access tokens Plajah stored for that account. You can also remove Plajah from the network directly: Facebook and Instagram under Settings → Business Integrations; X under Settings → Connected apps; LinkedIn under Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services. Removing Plajah there immediately stops it from posting.

Can’t sign in?

Email privacy@plajah.com from the address on your account, or tell us which Facebook or Instagram account you connected. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days and reply when it is done.
