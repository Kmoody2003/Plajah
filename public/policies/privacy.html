Plajah Privacy & Data Protection

Effective date: 2026-10-08

Overview

Plajah collects and processes personal data necessary to provide the service, including account information, uploaded content metadata, usage metrics, and billing details. We respect data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

Legal Bases

We rely on the following legal bases for processing:

  • Contractual necessity (to provide the Service and fulfill subscriptions).
  • Consent where required for optional features (e.g., marketing communications).
  • Legitimate interests for platform security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

User Rights (EU/UK)

EU and UK users may exercise rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. To exercise rights, contact privacy@plajah.com. We will respond within statutory timeframes.

Connected Social Accounts

Plajah’s Marketing tools let you connect accounts you own on Facebook (Pages), Instagram (Business or Creator accounts), X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon and Threads so you can publish and schedule posts from one place. When you connect an account, you sign in on that network’s own authorization screen and approve the permissions it lists. Plajah never sees your password for Facebook, Instagram, X or LinkedIn.

  • What we receive and store: an access token (and, where the network issues one, a refresh token), your account or Page name, handle, profile picture and public profile link. Tokens are stored encrypted, are used only by Plajah’s servers, and are never shown to your browser or shared with anyone else.
  • What we do with it: we publish the posts you create or schedule to the accounts you choose, at the time you choose, and show you whether each post succeeded. We do not post, like, follow, message or delete anything on your behalf unless you asked us to publish that post.
  • What we do not do: we do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We do not read your private messages or your followers’ personal information.
  • X: sharing to X can instead be done through X’s own share window, which opens X’s composer with your text filled in. In that case no X account is connected and no X credentials are stored.
  • Disconnecting: you can disconnect any account at any time from the Marketing tools, which deletes the stored tokens from Plajah. You can also revoke Plajah’s access from the network itself (Facebook and Instagram: Settings → Business Integrations; X: Settings → Connected apps; LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services). Deleting your Plajah account also deletes all stored tokens.
  • Data from Meta: data we receive from Facebook and Instagram is used only to provide the publishing features above, in line with Meta’s Platform Terms.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your Plajah account and associated data at any time. See the step-by-step instructions at plajah.com/policies/data-deletion.html, or email privacy@plajah.com.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including transaction history and legal obligations. Users may request deletion; account deletions remove profile data and uploaded content subject to backups and legal hold exceptions.

International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other jurisdictions. Where applicable, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and analytics to operate the Service and for product improvement. Users may manage cookie preferences via their browser or account settings.

Contact & Supervisory Authorities

Data protection questions or complaints can be directed to privacy@plajah.com. EU/UK data subjects may also contact their local supervisory authority.

Updates

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
