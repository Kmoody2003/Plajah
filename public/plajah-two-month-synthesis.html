7 Untapped Synergies & Hidden Superpowers

These seven workflows exist natively in the code today, but have not yet been packaged as front-facing commercial solutions. Each represents an enormous multiplier for creators, schools, and local businesses.

Synergy 01 · Local Commerce & Smart Ambience

The "Living Storefront" Loop

Business POS In-Store Live Lighting Designer Melos Ambience

A boutique, coffee shop, or church bookstore runs Plajah POS on a tablet. The background music streams from a curated Melos Ambient Soundscape or local artist channel. Over the store's Wi-Fi, Lighting Designer auto-detects Govee light strips and Nanoleaf panels, gently pulsing room lighting to the music. Customers walking in receive an automatic geofence notification opening In-Store Live: they see the song playing, can tip the musician or save it to their locker, and order items with instant loyalty discounts from their phone.

// The Physical Venue Revenue Loop
[Walk In] → Geofence triggers In-Store Live on customer phone
[In Store] → LD pulses Govee lights in sync with Melos soundscape
[Purchase] → POS register tenders cash/card + customer tips artist
Synergy 02 · Spatial Media & Architecture

Walkthrough to Spatial 3D Virtual Tour

Terra Listing Films Depth Anything V2 Spatial DIBR Shaders

A real estate agent walks through a newly completed Detroit building taking simple smartphone video while speaking aloud. Terra's listing film pipeline transcribes speech, identifies kitchen/living/bedroom rooms, and cuts an edited showcase. Passing this cut into Fabula's Depth Anything V2 engine generates a depth channel. Fabula's Spatial DIBR shader turns the flat video into a stereoscopic 3D experience playable directly on Apple Vision Pro and Meta Quest via WebXR.

// Automated Spatial Tour Pipeline
[Capture] → Smartphone video walkthrough with spoken narration
[Cut] → Terra room-detection cuts polished listing scenes
[Spatial 3D] → Depth Anything V2 + DIBR shader outputs WebXR headset tour

Synergy 06 · Rights & Automated Micro-Licensing

Audio Sample Clearance to Video Revenue Splits

Melos Rights Engine Fabula Timeline Stripe Order Spine

A beatmaker in Melos flags a custom drum pattern as cleared for sampling. A filmmaker in Fabula searches the Universal Library, finds the groove, and drops it into their film timeline. The license is automatically bonded. When the movie premieres on Live TV+ FAST or PPV, the Stripe order spine splits revenue automatically: 70% to the director, 20% to the music producer, and 10% platform fee—zero DMCA strikes or lawyer retainers.

// Frictionless Derivative Licensing
[Publish] → Beatmaker clears sample in Melos with predefined split rate
[Sync] → Video creator drops stem into Fabula with automated rights bond
[Payout] → Ad/ticket revenue splits automatically on the Stripe spine
