What it taps into

// one asset service, five shelves

The library is a window onto Plajah's asset-management service. The same panel resolves the assets you own, the ones your team or business shares, licensed stock, and what the community makes public — plus every preset, template and effect built into the platform.

The Fabula media retrofit

// pool vs. library

Fabula's right side becomes the Universal Library. The Media Pool stops being a catch-all and becomes exactly what a Resolve pool is — the contents of this project. The import, sync and music actions move to a dedicated Media page.
