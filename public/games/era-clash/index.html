Pair an Xbox controller over Bluetooth or USB — its native layout is picked up automatically. The first pad fights as Player 1; plug in a second and it becomes Player 2 in Local Versus.

Best of three rounds, 60 seconds each in duels; Campaign battles end when the field is won or lost. Blocking blunts almost all damage. Landing and taking hits charges your super meter. Every warrior knows seven specials from Level 1: Flurry Jab, Haymaker, Roundhouse, Axe Kick, Rising Uppercut, Low Sweep/Throw, and Dash — see the Move List on the character select and pause screens. Attacks you tap a beat early are buffered and fire the moment you're free. On phones and tablets the touch controls appear during the match: left thumb moves (hold ◀ ▶, tap ▲ to jump, DASH to dash), right thumb fights (PUNCH, KICK, BLK, FLUR, HAYM, RNDH, AXEK, UPC, SWP, plus WPN and SUPER as they unlock). They are multi-touch — hold a direction and tap an attack at the same time. Hold the phone sideways for the biggest arena, and use Full Screen for an unobstructed view. In online duels the host's screen runs the match — a steady connection keeps it smooth.

In Campaign, the same moves carry you through battlefield brawls: you fight as one warrior beside two companions against waves of foes. Clear every wave to take the field and reach the next checkpoint. On touch devices the thumb controls work exactly as in a duel.
