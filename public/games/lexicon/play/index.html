How to Play & Controller Map

Something is unwriting the world. You are the Scribe: win the words back, reclaim white-paper Infected Zones, and explore the wilderness.
Controller Mapping (Xbox-Style · Works on ALL Controllers)
  1. Talk & Lasso your first word. Talk to Elder Mira (E / A), walk toward the golden word drifting over the stone bridge, and press C / RB to cast your Word Lasso.
  2. Reclaim Infected Zones. Once you wake your first word, the Unwriting turns infected areas to white paper and sends packs of enemies. Strike with J / X, answer with 1–4 or D-Pad to rescue words, and clear the pack to watch living colour bloom back across the land!
  3. Explore the Waterfall & Deep Vaults. Follow the cobbled creek west to the Roaring Triple Waterfall and climb behind the veil into the Crystal Grotto — solve word, reflection, gear-ratio, and torque vaults, ride the Archive Mine-Cart, and survive the Forest Rapids!
  4. Ride the Great Forest Elk. Beyond the rapids, summon and bond with the cyan-antlered Great Forest Elk (V / D-Pad ▲ to mount/call, Space / A to leap logs, J / X Antler Surge, C / RB mounted Word Lasso).
  5. Play Songs & Face the Redactor. Press Q / Y for the Lyre (LIGHT, TIME, RAIN, GROW, OPEN, MEND) and weigh your saved words at the Cipher Gate (letter values printed right on every tile, scalable for all ages!) to enter the Temple of Numerals.
